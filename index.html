Energetica Exhibition on Loan

On loan from the NEMO Science Museum in Amsterdam, the Energetica exhibition comes to Community Science Museum.

It’s a series of installations that allow visitors to experience the power of them.

From solar energy powering lighting, to ‘Wind Island’ that shows how turbines are able to use and control wind to create power.
